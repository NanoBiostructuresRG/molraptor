# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers, we pledge to make participation in
**MOLRAPTOR** and its community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

We are committed to an open, respectful, inclusive, and professional
environment for scientific and software collaboration.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- the use of sexualized language or imagery;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and for taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to
unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull
requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including the repository,
issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project communication channels.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing MOLRAPTOR or its
community in public, including through an official project account or as an
appointed representative at an online or in-person event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately by email to **Flavio F. Contreras-Torres**, the project
maintainer, at <contreras.flavio@tec.mx>.

Please do not use public GitHub issues for sensitive conduct reports.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with appropriate respect for the
privacy and safety of the people involved.
